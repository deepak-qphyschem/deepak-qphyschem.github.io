About Me

I am a Theoretical and Computational Chemistry researcher specializing in DFT, with a focus on the rational design of quantum materials and SACs for hydrogen energy applications. My expertise encompasses electronic structure calculations, reaction kinetics, and molecular dynamics simulations of nanomaterials and biomolecular systems. I also work on multiscale modelling and fracture mechanics of nanocomposites, investigating their dynamic response and failure behaviour across length scales. My research integrates atomistic simulations and predictive modelling to advance both sustainable energy technologies and high-performance structural materials.
